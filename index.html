01 · The grill

SMALL KITCHEN.
BIG BURGERS.

Tierney's keeps the food direct: burgers, grill classics, sandwiches and bar sides. The famous Buddy Burger is the order people talk about—ask the bartender or server for it.

From the kitchen

Tavern classics, made in a mighty little grill.

No oversized menu system—just the items people come to a neighborhood bar for.

  • Buddy Burger
  • Burgers & cheeseburgers
  • Wings & chicken fingers
  • Cheesesteak
  • Corned beef & pastrami
  • Grilled cheese
  • Turkey clubs & BLTs
  • Fries & onion rings
Kitchen notice: Tierney's uses one shared flat top and two shared fryers, cannot guarantee freedom from allergens already present on the menu, and cooks with lard. Tell staff about allergies before ordering.
