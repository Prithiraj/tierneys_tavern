## Production photography still required

The collected images are sufficient for planning, but a public launch should commission or obtain owner-cleared versions of:

1. Blue-hour front and corner exterior
2. Straight-on façade coverage for photogrammetry
3. Roofline, doors, signage, windows, brick, plaster, and timber details
4. Downstairs bar empty and active
5. Upstairs room in band, comedy, standing, and seated arrangements
6. Buddy Burger preparation and finished plate
7. Beer pour and grill action
8. Tierney family portrait
9. Historical photographs, menus, receipts, signs, and memorabilia
10. Merchandise on neutral and environmental backgrounds

These are acquisition requirements, not image placeholders. The design must not substitute unrelated stock imagery.
